## Brand & Style

This design system drives an experimental, mathematically rigorous laboratory exploring the psychological dynamics of academic pressure through Set Theory, Inclusion-Exclusion principles, and Propositional Logic. The interface lives at the intersection of modern academic pedagogy and playful 2026 product craft—merging the irreverent curiosity of experimental indie software with the analytical legibility of pure mathematics.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Intellectually Playful:** Demystifies high-stakes stress metrics via interactive, tactile math primitives (Venn logic sets, dynamic boolean toggles, probabilistic sliders).
- **Punchy & Expressive:** Deploys saturated, hyper-distinctive hues balanced over warm editorial cream and grounded by deep ink linework. It avoids sterile corporate dashboard aesthetics in favor of a vibrant, living workbook.
- **Analytical Precision:** Crisp delineations, strict typographic hierarchies, and distinct visual groupings that treat logic equations with equal reverence to lived student experience.

### Visual Style
A hybrid of **Neo-Brutalism, Modern Bento Modularism, and Tactile Academic Graphic Design**. It leverages 1.5px to 2px structural ink outlines, intentional asymmetry, bold color-blocked logic zones, high-density data metrics, and pill-shaped interactive anchors. Whitespace is generous but dynamic, allowing multi-set overlapping diagrams to breathe against stark numeric statistics.

## Colors

The palette embraces high chrominance, sharp semantic differentiation, and uncompromising readability. Each stress axis and set-theoretic operator maps to an explicit chromatic identity.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary — Electric Blue (`#4057FF`):** Represents logic foundations, propositional proofs, primary interaction states, and focus rings.
- **Secondary — Tangerine Coral (`#FF735E`):** Represents high urgency, elevated anxiety indices, and stress vector $P$ (Procrastination).
- **Tertiary — Acid Lime (`#D9FF66`):** Signals validation, predicted equilibrium, intersection sets, and high-contrast callout triggers. Always paired with Deep Ink text.
- **Neutral — Deep Ink (`#201B31`):** Structural borders, high-contrast typography, glyphs, and hard outline frames.
- **Surface Warm Cream (`#FFF8E9`):** The primary canvas surface, replacing cold digital grays with a warm, risograph-inspired academic paper feel.

### Accent & Logic Set Color Tokens
- **Set $S$ (Sleep Deficit / Lavender):** `#BDA7FF`
- **Set $C$ (Social Pressure / Bubblegum Pink):** `#FF91D1`
- **Intersection Neutralizer / Turquoise:** `#67E8D0`
- **Pure Surface Container:** `#FFFFFF` (used inside bento tiles to float over the warm cream canvas).

### Color Application Guidelines
- Maintain WCAG AAA compliance on all body copy by rendering text strictly in Deep Ink (`#201B31`) over Warm Cream, Pure White, or Acid Lime surfaces.
- In multi-set Venn and truth-table visualizers, set components rely on `mix-blend-mode: multiply` at 70–85% opacity to dynamically expose real-time overlapping intersections.

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts the expressive, eccentric geometry of **Space Grotesk** with the neutral, hyper-legible engineering of **Geist**.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Character
- **Headlines & Display Numbers:** Powered by Space Grotesk. Features condensed numerals and idiosyncratic ink-traps that give statistical correlations and formula notations a striking, contemporary identity.
- **Body & Data Displays:** Powered by Geist. Ensures that propositional expressions (e.g., $P \land \neg S \implies C$), truth matrices, and explanatory psychological text remain legible even at small sizes.
- **Formulas & Notations:** Mathematical tokens inherit `code-formula` with explicit monospace lining figures to prevent horizontal jitter during interactive slider recalculations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured **Modular Bento Grid** system, balancing dense dynamic cards with breathing room between analytical sectors.

### Grid System
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Complex bento blocks collapse into vertical, sequentially scrollable modules.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout. Truth tables and set diagrams pair side-by-side with prediction inputs.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column asymmetric layout (e.g., 7-column primary interactive sandbox paired with a 5-column live truth-table & live outcome feed). Max-width container is clamped at 1440px to preserve visual density.

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing is governed by an 8pt base grid rhythm. Interior card padding uses `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) for mobile. Gaps between distinct thematic bento modules prioritize `space-lg` or `space-xl` to maintain structural rhythm and visual separation.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids blurred, ethereal elevation in favor of **Crisp Geometric Separation and Tactile Micro-Offsets**.

### Stacking & Depth Strategies
- **Primary Ink Borders:** Structural separation is driven by a uniform `1.5px solid #201B31` perimeter on all tiles, inputs, and interactive surfaces.
- **Offset Block Drop (Tactile Depth):** High-priority interactive elements (e.g., CTA buttons, active cards, draggable nodes) utilize hard, unblurred drop-shadow offsets:
  - Default elevation: `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #201B31;`
  - Active/Pressed elevation: `box-shadow: 1px 1px 0px #201B31; transform: translate(2px, 2px);`
  - Static Bento tiles: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px rgba(32, 27, 49, 0.08);`
- **Surface Stacking:** Warm Cream (`#FFF8E9`) acts as ground level 0; Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) forms level 1 interactive tiles; Tinted highlight cards (Electric Blue, Acid Lime, Lavender) form level 2 focal overlays.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a coherent `roundedness: 2` scale, balancing structural rectangularity with inviting, modern rounded corners.

### Radii Application
- **Standard Cards, Bento Tiles & Math Blocks:** Default to `0.5rem` (8px) border radius for crisp architectural alignment.
- **Interactive Badges, Pill Filters & Micro-Chips:** Use full circular pills (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to visually differentiate data attributes from modular structural containers.
- **Interactive Set Sliders & Toggle Controls:** Knobs and indicators rely on circular or pill geometry to encourage touch exploration.
- **Venn Nodes:** Elliptical geometries strictly retain continuous circular curvature with 1.5px ink outlines.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Background `#4057FF`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #201B31`, radius `0.5rem`. Tactile hard offset `3px 3px 0px #201B31`. Hover reduces offset; active state presses flat.
- **Secondary (Formula Toggle):** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#201B31`, border `1.5px solid #201B31`. Hover triggers `#D9FF66` tint.
- **Touch Target:** Minimum height of 48px across mobile breakpoints.

### 2. Rounded Pill Badges & Chips
- Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`), 1.5px deep ink border, compact text (`label-md`).
- Color coded by set-membership:
  - Procrastination: `#FF735E` background, `#201B31` text.
  - Sleep Deficit: `#BDA7FF` background, `#201B31` text.
  - Social Pressure: `#FF91D1` background, `#201B31` text.
  - Intersection / Balanced: `#67E8D0` background, `#201B31` text.

### 3. Truth-Table Cards & Formula Blocks
- Contained within `#FFFFFF` cards with a `1.5px solid #201B31` outline.
- Header row styled in `#201B31` with `#FFF8E9` monospaced headings.
- Matrix cells alternate soft zebra fills using `rgba(32, 27, 49, 0.03)` with dynamic status cells highlighting true values in `#D9FF66` and false values in `#FF735E`.

### 4. Interactive Prediction Sliders
- Track thickness: 8px solid track with `#201B31` background and filled active progress in `#4057FF` or `#FF735E`.
- Thumb: 24px wide circle or squircle, background `#FFFFFF`, border `2px solid #201B31`, shadow `2px 2px 0px #201B31`.

### 5. Interactive Venn Diagram Container
- Contained inside a bento canvas tile with Warm Cream backdrop.
- Overlapping logic sets use translucent fills (`#FF735E`, `#BDA7FF`, `#67E8D0`) with multiply blend modes and continuous 1.5px solid Ink perimeters.
- Intersection areas respond to hover/drag with an active `#D9FF66` stroke outline and tooltip revealing the formal Set equation: $|A \cup B \cup C|$.

### 6. Research Statistic Bento Card
- Large-format metrics displaying prediction vs. reality discrepancy rates.
- Composed of an oversized `stat-counter` Space Grotesk number anchored alongside a descriptive label and mini sparkline or boolean indicator tag.
