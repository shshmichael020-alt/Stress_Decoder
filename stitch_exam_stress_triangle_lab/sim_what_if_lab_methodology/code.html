Methodology & Framework

4 Modules

For any finite sets A, B, and C, simple summation over-counts elements belonging to multiple sets. The formal identity enforces:

|A ∪ B ∪ C| = |A| + |B| + |C| - (|A ∩ B| + |A ∩ C| + |B ∩ C|) + |A ∩ B ∩ C|

Proof Intuition: Summing |A| + |B| + |C| counts triple-overlap elements three times. Subtracting all pairwise intersections subtracts triple-overlap elements three times (leaving zero representation). Thus, adding back |A ∩ B ∩ C| exactly once produces the proper cardinality of their union.

In classical Boolean logic, the material conditional statement (P ∧ S ∧ F) → E operates under absolute falsification criteria. A single student instance exhibiting high procrastination, severe sleep deprivation, and extreme social pressure without developing acute exam stress (i.e., True → False) mathematically invalidates the implication as a universal theorem.

In psychological sciences, however, empirical relations are stochastic. The coexistence of counterexamples does not repudiate predictive utility; it quantifies resilience factors and unmeasured interaction effects.

Continuous psychological survey dimensions are categorized into discrete Set Membership via standardized Likert binarization:

  • Set P (Procrastination): Score ≥ 16/20 on the 5-item Academic Delay Scale.
  • Set S (Sleep Deficit): Reported self-sleep < 5.5 hours/night for ≥ 4 consecutive days prior to evaluation.
  • Set F (Social Strain): "Often" or "Almost Always" on at least 3 peer/familial expectation indices.
  • Set E (Acute Exam Stress): Composite State-Trait Anxiety Inventory (STAI) equivalent ≥ 62nd percentile.

Cross-sectional survey instruments introduce retrospective recall bias and demand characteristics. Observational set theory reveals structural correlations and non-disjoint cluster patterns, but cannot establish unidirectional temporal causality.

External confounds including course credit loads, caffeine intake, and pre-existing psychiatric conditions represent unpartitioned latent variables.
